Background and reference material for the demonstration of cross-border branch registration in the WE BUILD pilot.

Terms

EUDI Wallet
European Digital Identity Wallet. A wallet under the eIDAS2 Regulation in which a person holds and presents electronic attestations of attributes.
EBW
European Business Wallet. A wallet for organisations to exchange verified data and documents, as proposed in the draft EBW regulation.
PID
Person Identification Data. The attestation that identifies a natural person and is issued to the EUDI Wallet.
EU PoA
EU Power of Attorney. An attestation that a natural person is authorised to represent a company.
EUCC
EU Company Certificate. An attestation of the company’s data from the business register in its home Member State.
EUID
European Unique Identifier. The identifier that identifies a company across the business registers of the Member States.
(Q)EAA
(Qualified) Electronic Attestation of Attributes. The type of attestation that PID, EU PoA and EUCC are issued as.
Home Member State
The Member State where the company is registered. It issues the power of attorney and the EUCC.
Host Member State
The Member State where the branch is registered. It receives the attestations and registers the branch.
