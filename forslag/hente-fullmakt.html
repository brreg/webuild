Step 2

Obtain power of attorney (EU PoA)

The person registered as authorised representative in step 1 now obtains their power of attorney as an electronic attestation of attributes.

How to do it

  1. The authorised representative logs in to ID-porten
  2. Select International as the login method.
  3. Select Log in with email.
  4. Enter your email address, then the one-time code sent to that address.
  5. Use the code “12345” and continue.
  6. Scan the QR code to obtain the EU PoA.
  7. Approve the request in the wallet.
  8. On the web page, confirm that you have shared data from the wallet.
  9. Approve receipt of the EU PoA in the wallet.
The EU PoA should now be stored in the wallet.
