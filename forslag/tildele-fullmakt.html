Step 1

Grant power of attorney (EU PoA)

Register a power of attorney to represent a Norwegian company when registering a branch in another country.

How to do it

The person who is to grant the power of attorney on behalf of the company must be registered with the role of CEO (daglig leder) in the company. Select a test company to see information about the company and the persons associated with it. Test company lookup

  1. Open Form for registering a power of attorney (EU PoA)
  2. You are taken to the ID-porten login page.
  3. Switch the language to English.
  4. Select TestID på nivå høyt under Norwegian login.
  5. Enter the person identifier (national identity number) of the CEO in the field Person identifier (synthetic).
  6. Click Authenticate.
  7. You are now logged in to Altinn and see an overview of the companies you are registered as CEO for. Select the company for which the EU PoA is to be issued.
  8. Fill in the form Register EU-PoA. Information about the company and the logged-in CEO is pre-filled. Enter the following information about the authorised representative:
    • Name
    • Date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY)
    • Personal adm number
  9. Specify what the power of attorney covers.
  10. Enter the expiry date of the power of attorney (MM/DD/YYYY).
  11. Sign and submit. You will receive a receipt confirming that the power of attorney has been created.
  12. Open the menu in the top right corner and log out.
The power of attorney (EU PoA) should now be created.
