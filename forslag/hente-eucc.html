Step 3

Obtain EUCC

The authorised representative obtains the company's EUCC as an electronic attestation of attributes.

How to do it

  1. Log in to ID-porten. Use the email address you registered in step 2.
  2. Select International as the login method.
  3. Select Log in with email.
  4. Enter your email address, then the one-time code sent to that address.
  5. Use the code “12345” and continue.
  6. Enter the EUID and name of the Norwegian company that granted the power of attorney (EU PoA). Test company lookup
  7. Scan the QR code.
  8. Approve the request in the wallet.
The EUCC should now be stored in the wallet. Continue by registering the branch in the other country’s own solution.
