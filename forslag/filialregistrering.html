Step 1

Carry out branch registration

The authorised representative of the foreign company carries out the registration of a Norwegian branch. The authorised representative must hold the following attestations in their EUDI Wallet, obtained in the company’s home Member State: PID, EU PoA and EUCC for the foreign company.

How to do it

  1. Log in to ID-porten.
  2. The authorised representative with a Norwegian EUDI Wallet selects Digital lommebok (digital wallet) as the login method under Norwegian login.
