Step 1

Carry out branch registration

The authorised representative of the foreign company carries out the registration of a Norwegian branch. The authorised representative must hold the following attestations in their EUDI Wallet, obtained in the company’s home Member State: PID, EU PoA and EUCC for the foreign company.

The authorised representative must have a Norwegian national identity number.

How to do it

  1. Open the form for registering a branch.
  2. You are taken to a login page for ID-porten.
  3. Change the language to English.
  4. Login method: select Digital lommebok (digital wallet) as the login method under Norwegian login.
  5. Alternative login method: select TestID på nivå høyt under Norwegian login. Enter the Norwegian person identifier of the person who has been issued the EU PoA in the field Person Identifier (synthetic). Click Authenticate.
  6. You are now logged in to Altinn and see an overview of who you can represent. Choose to represent yourself. (👤)
  7. Scan the QR code to share the PID, EU PoA and EUCC. Share the attestations from the wallet, then confirm in the browser that the attestations have been shared.
  8. Fill in the form for registering a Norwegian branch of a foreign company. Information about the foreign company is pre-filled from the EUCC. Answer two questions about the foreign company and fill in the information about the Norwegian branch.
    • The name of the branch is pre-filled in accordance with Norwegian law.
    • Business address of the branch: enter a street address (no field validation) and a postcode (validated as a valid Norwegian postcode). These can be used: 8900, 5302, 0123.
    • Postal address of the branch: optional.
    • Activity of the branch: select a NACE code from the list.
    • Branch manager (daglig leder): the person must have a Norwegian person identifier. Enter the person’s person identifier and surname. Test person lookup
  9. Continue to signing. You see an overview of the information that will be submitted.
  10. Sign and submit. You receive a receipt confirming that the registration form has been submitted.
